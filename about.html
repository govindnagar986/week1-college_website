About SoIT

The School of Information Technology (SoIT) is a university teaching department of Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV).

SoIT focuses on technical education, computer science, information technology, research and innovation.

History of SoIT

The School of Information Technology was established in 2002-03 at RGPV, Bhopal.

Since its establishment, the school has contributed to technical education and research in information technology and related fields.

Our Vision

To achieve academic excellence in the digital era and develop socially responsible professionals with a global outlook.

Our Mission

  • To provide a lifelong learning environment.
  • To strengthen technical knowledge and core competencies.
  • To encourage innovation and problem-solving skills.
  • To prepare students for the challenges of the technology industry.

Academic Environment

SoIT provides an academic environment focused on learning, technical knowledge, research and professional development.

Students are encouraged to explore emerging technologies and develop practical problem-solving abilities.

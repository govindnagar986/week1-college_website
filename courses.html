School of Information Technology (SoIT)

Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya, Bhopal

Academic Programs

SoIT focuses on technology-oriented education and provides opportunities to learn computer science, information technology and emerging technologies.

Courses Offered

B.Tech in Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS)

This program combines computer science concepts with business knowledge and problem-solving skills.

B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (AI & ML)

This program focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning and intelligent computer systems.

B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science)

This program focuses on data analysis, statistics and extracting useful information from data.

M.Tech in Data Science

This postgraduate program focuses on advanced data analysis, research and data-driven technologies.

Learning Objectives

  • Develop programming and technical skills.
  • Understand computer science fundamentals.
  • Improve analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Explore modern technologies and research.

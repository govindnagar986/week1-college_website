Research Areas at SoIT

The School of Information Technology focuses on various areas of computer science, information technology and emerging technologies.

Our Research Areas

1. Systems and Networking

Study of computer systems, communication networks and distributed computing.

2. Security and Privacy

Focuses on protecting computer systems, networks and digital information.

3. Programming Languages and Software Engineering

Study of programming languages, software development and formal methods.

4. Databases and Data Mining

Focuses on storing, managing, analysing and discovering useful patterns from data.

5. Mobile Computing

Study of mobile devices, wireless communication and mobile applications.

6. Theory and Algorithms

Focuses on computational theory, algorithm design and problem-solving.

7. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Study of intelligent systems that learn from data and perform tasks.

8. Human-Computer Interaction and Information Visualization

Focuses on improving interaction between humans and computer systems and presenting information visually.

9. Vision and Graphics

Study of computer vision, image processing and computer graphics.

10. Robotics

Focuses on robotic systems, automation and intelligent machines.
